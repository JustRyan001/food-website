Our Story

Born in Naples,
Loved Worldwide

In 1987, Chef Marco Rossi carried his grandmother's centuries-old recipe from a tiny kitchen in Naples to the world stage. Every pizza we make is a love letter to tradition — stretched by hand, fired in a 900°F wood oven, and topped with ingredients we source directly from the volcanic soils of Campania.

We don't rush. Great pizza takes time, passion, and a generous pour of olive oil. Welcome to Paradise.
